## checks

## connector correction

amass's current drawing for c98732 specifies two 0.8×1.9mm mounting slots; the imported footprint used 0.6×1.6mm. the board and local footprint were corrected together, with 1.4×2.5mm copper lands preserving the original 0.3mm annulus. the top edge extends 0.15mm to retain the existing 0.5mm copper-edge clearance. connector position, pin pitch and all electrical nets remain unchanged. after these changes, fresh saved-file erc, refilled drc, parity and the updated verifier again passed with zero violations, opens or exclusions. the driver models now reference the bundled step file instead of a missing temporary path. the xt60 model preview is approximate; fit was checked against the [exact c98732 drawing](https://datasheet.lcsc.com/datasheet/pdf/e258adf1ce44622bb05a5787d35fc0fa.pdf?productCode=C98732). final checks and manufacturing hashes are in `../../build/review/pr5-72f5b65/`.
